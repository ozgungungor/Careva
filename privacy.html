Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Özgün Güngör built the Careva app as a Commercial app. This SERVICE is provided by Özgün Güngör and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide and improve our Service, we collect certain personally identifiable information and vehicle data. The types of data we collect include:

  • Location Data: Precise and coarse location data to map driving routes, track vehicle position, and identify nearby charging stations.
  • Identifiers & Contact Info: Your email address (via Sign in with Apple), User IDs, and Device/Vehicle IDs (VIN) for account management and secure API connections.
  • Usage & Telemetry Data: Vehicle status, battery health, charging sessions, speed, and idle drain statistics required for app functionality.
  • Purchases: Purchase history and subscription status (via Apple StoreKit) to unlock Careva Pro features. We do not collect or store your credit card information.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs and performance data to maintain server stability and troubleshoot errors.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Specifically:

  • Apple: For secure authentication (Sign in with Apple) and processing In-App Purchases.
  • Tesla: For direct vehicle telemetry integration and executing vehicle commands via official APIs. We do not store your Tesla account passwords; authentication relies on secure tokens.

3. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Careva offers auto-renewing subscriptions (e.g., Monthly and Yearly Pro plans) to access premium features. Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at the confirmation of purchase. Subscription automatically renews unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel your subscriptions by going to your App Store account settings.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. Your data is stored securely on our servers and linked to your account solely for app functionality. We do not sell your data or use it for third-party tracking/advertising.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
